How do I play Lanbit Memory?

🎮 HOW IT WORKS

1. Type your name: Start by registering your alias for the leaderboard.

2. Flip cards: All cards have a Lanbit logo on the back. Click a logo to see the image.

3. Find pairs: Click a second card. If the images match, they stay flipped. If they are different, they flip back after one second.

🟦 CASUAL (Training)

  • Time: Counts upwards from 00.00.00[cite: 3].
  • Traps: None. Kamek acts as a normal pair[cite: 3].
  • Goal: Fastest time wins the tournament!
  • Gold Coin: Win one if you get the rating "EXCELLENT!"[cite: 3].

🟥 CLASSIC (Challenge)

  • Time: Counts downwards (Timer). If time runs out, you lose[cite: 3].
  • Bonus: Every correct pair gives +5 seconds[cite: 3].
  • Traps: Kamek shuffles, Bowser deducts time, and NUKE is a disaster[cite: 3]!
  • Star: Win the star on EXTREME[cite: 3]!
SECURITY: Security Crew Psl631 is monitoring the station. No cheating (F12/Right-Click/Blur) – Kamek will lock the game immediately and laugh at you with kamek.mp3[cite: 3]!
